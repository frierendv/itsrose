### 1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how **ItsRose** ("ItsRose", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you access or use our website, developer dashboard, REST APIs, software development kits (SDKs), documentation, and related developer tooling (collectively, the **"Service"** or **"Platform"**).

By registering for an account, generating an API key, or accessing our APIs, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use the Service.

For the legal terms governing your use of the Platform, please review our [Terms of Service](/terms).

### 2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to operate our developer platform, enforce security protocols, manage API rate limits, and provide customer support. The information we collect falls into the following categories:

#### A. Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information:** When you create an account, we collect registration details such as your email address, username, password hash, and profile details.
- **Authentication Credentials:** If you authenticate via third-party OAuth providers (e.g., Google Sign-In), we receive authentication tokens, unique identifier tokens, and associated profile information permitted by your provider settings.
- **Communications and Support:** When you submit support tickets, reach out via our contact channels, or participate in platform surveys, we collect the contents of your messages and contact details.

#### B. Technical and Telemetry Data (Collected Automatically)

- **API Request Telemetry:** When you make requests to our API endpoints, we log request metadata, including the requesting IP address, HTTP method, endpoint route, status codes, response latencies, timestamps, user-agent strings, and API key identifiers.
- **System and Security Diagnostics:** Error stack traces, crash reports, firewall event triggers, and rate-limiting metrics collected to maintain system stability and prevent abuse.
- **Device and Browser Information:** Standard browser headers, operating system information, language settings, and screen resolution when accessing the dashboard interface.

#### C. API Request Payloads & Processing Inputs

- **Customer Inputs:** Text prompts, parameters, media URLs, image files, audio buffers, or structured data submitted via API endpoints to execute tasks (e.g., image upscaling, audio generation, data extraction, or transformation).
- **Processing Metadata:** Content type, payload size, execution duration, and computational resource utilization.

#### D. Billing and Transaction Metadata

- **Transaction Details:** When subscribing to paid tiers or purchasing API credits, we collect billing identifiers, plan subscriptions, invoice history, credit allocations, and transaction timestamps.
- **Payment Processing:** All direct payment processing (such as credit card numbers, billing addresses, and CVVs) is handled directly by our certified, PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment processors. ItsRose does not store raw payment card data on its servers.

### 3. How We Use Information

We process personal information and telemetry for the following operational and business purposes:

- **Service Delivery & API Execution:** Authenticating API requests, processing customer payloads, executing transformations, and delivering responses.
- **Quota & Billing Management:** Metering endpoint consumption, tracking credit balances, processing renewals, and enforcing tier-based rate limits.
- **Platform Security & Fraud Prevention:** Identifying abnormal traffic spikes, detecting automated attacks, investigating credential misuse, and mitigating DDoS or unauthorized API exploitation.
- **Customer Support & Communications:** Responding to technical inquiries, notifying developers of service advisories, maintenance windows, billing receipts, and critical security notices.
- **Platform Optimization & Reliability:** Monitoring infrastructure performance, capacity planning, identifying bottlenecks, and improving API responsiveness.
- **Compliance & Legal Obligations:** Enforcing our Terms of Service and complying with statutory obligations, court orders, and lawful regulatory requests.

### 4. API Payloads, Ephemeral Processing & AI Specifics

We recognize the sensitivity of developer data and user-submitted media. Our data processing architecture follows strict confidentiality standards:

- **Ephemeral In-Memory Processing:** User-submitted media, audio, and prompt inputs are processed in-memory or cached transiently in edge computing layers solely for the duration required to complete the API request and deliver the result.
- **No Unauthorized Model Training:** We do not use your private API request payloads, uploaded files, or generated outputs to train, fine-tune, or improve public foundation AI models without your explicit prior authorization.
- **Subprocessor Inference:** Where specialized inference models or upstream processing nodes are utilized to fulfill endpoint requests, data transfers are strictly secured via encrypted channels and bound by strict confidentiality and processing agreements.
- **Data Isolation:** Developer accounts and API keys are logically segregated to prevent data leakage between different users and organizations.

### 5. Cookies and Local Storage

We use cookies, local storage, and similar session technologies strictly for necessary functionality and developer convenience:

- **Essential Authentication Cookies:** Maintain authenticated sessions, protect against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF), and verify active login status.
- **Preference Cookies:** Store user preferences, including dark/light theme selection, sidebar visibility, and dashboard interface configurations.
- **No Intrusive Ad Tracking:** We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies or behavioral cross-site tracking scripts.

You can manage or disable cookies via your browser settings; however, disabling essential session cookies will prevent you from accessing authenticated areas of the dashboard.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or API payload data. We only share information under the following limited circumstances:

- **With Authorized Service Providers:** Third-party subprocessors acting on our behalf to operate the platform under strict confidentiality agreements.

- **Business Transfers:** In the event of a merger, acquisition, corporate reorganization, or sale of platform assets, customer data may be transferred to the acquiring entity subject to this Privacy Policy.

### 8. Data Security and Safeguards

We implement comprehensive technical, administrative, and physical security measures to safeguard your data:

- **Encryption in Transit:** All traffic between your client applications, our dashboard, and our API endpoints is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
- **Encryption at Rest:** Account data, authentication records, and persistent configurations are stored with AES-256 encryption.
- **API Key Protection:** API keys are hashed and masked in the developer interface. Secret keys are displayed in full only upon initial creation.
- **Access Controls:** Internal access to production infrastructure is restricted under the principle of least privilege (PoLP) and protected by multi-factor authentication (MFA).

While we employ industry-standard safeguards, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely impenetrable. We encourage developers to safeguard their API keys and avoid exposing secrets in public repositories.

### 9. Data Retention and Account Deletion

- **Account Data:** Retained for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with access to the Platform.
- **API Logs & Telemetry:** Request metadata and server diagnostics are retained for a rolling window (typically 30 to 90 days) for security auditing, fraud detection, and performance analysis, after which they are permanently deleted or anonymized.
- **Account Deletion:** You can request account deletion at any time through the dashboard or by contacting support. Upon account termination, your personal data and API keys are permanently purged from active systems within 30 days, except where retention is required by tax, billing, or legal mandates.

### 10. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA & Global)

Depending on your geographic location, you may have specific privacy rights under applicable data protection regulations (such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation and the California Consumer Privacy Act):

- **Right to Access & Portability:** Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Right to Rectification:** Update or correct inaccurate or incomplete account details directly through your dashboard.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"):** Request the permanent deletion of your personal data and account records.
- **Right to Restriction & Objection:** Object to or request restriction of certain processing activities under specific legal circumstances.
- **Non-Discrimination:** We will never deny services, charge different rates, or degrade quality for exercising your statutory privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, submit a request via our support channels or dashboard settings. We will verify your identity and respond within the statutory timeframe (typically 30 days).
